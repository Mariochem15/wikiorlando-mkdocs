    Getting Started¶

    This section aims to provide structured sections to make resources from multiple community based-organizations, the City of Orlando, and other entities as accessible as possible to navigate.

    Essential Support and Resources for Facing Hardships¶

    • If you're dealing with any hardships, these resources bellow are here to help you during difficult times. You can find shelters, food aid, healthcare, and other essential resources to help you regain stability.

    Important

    • If you're having an Emergerency, please call 911
    • If you're experiencing emotional distress or mental health struggles, please call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    • For more resources and support, call 211
    • For Orange County support, call 311

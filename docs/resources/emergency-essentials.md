Life is an unpredictable journey filled with various challenges. If you're facing hardships, these resources bellow are here to help you during these difficult times. Here, you can find shelters, food aid, healthcare, and other essential resources. Remember, you're not alone and will come out stronger on the other side.   

!!! warning "Important" 

    - If you're having an Emergency, please call **911**  
    - If you're experiencing emotional distress or mental health struggles, please call **988** for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    - For City of Orlando resources and support, call **211**  
    - For Orange County support, call **311**

> Provides one-time financial assistance for rent, mortgage, and/or utilities (electric, gas, or water only) for individuals and families with an unforeseen loss of income or increase in expense(s) that are recently past due on their housing or basic utilities and will be self-sufficient within the next month.

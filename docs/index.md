# Welcome to WikiOrlando


WikiOrlando exists to help new residents in the Orlando area who arent aware of the resources available to them. This resource focuses on making web content more accessible and easier to find. 

Resources are available in both English and Spanish by hovering and clicking on the __ button at the top of the page.
